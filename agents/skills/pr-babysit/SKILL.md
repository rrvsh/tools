# PR babysitting

1. Wait for all CI and automated AI review checks.
2. Address every valid AI review finding by updating the PR.
  2.1. Ignore invalid AI review findings.
3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 until the AI review reports only invalid or no issues.
4. Reply to and resolve every AI review thread.
